# Operator walkthrough of the PMC screen

Rev A, 13 Oct 2026. Basis: WP023 s6.1 p15 (prototype with users) and s8.11.5 p28 (test with realistic tasks).

## Set-up

- Two operators, separately, about 15 minutes each.
- The simulator on a laptop or tablet with **Physical size** pressed. Check with a ruler that the screen is about 95 mm wide, and adjust the browser zoom if it is not.
- One observer writes notes and does not help unless an operator is stuck for more than 30 s.
- Place the screen at the distance and angle it will have on the instrument, with something else to read (standing in for the temperature controller) at the operator's usual position.

## Tasks

Read each task aloud. Ask the operator to think aloud.

1. Press Reboot board. "The controller has just been powered. Get it ready to regulate."
2. "Hold the pot at 5 mbar."
3. Wait 2 minutes. While the operator reads the other display, ask: "Glance at the PMC. Is it holding steady? How do you know?"
4. "Change to 4 mbar."
5. While the operator looks away, the observer unplugs the Druck on the bench panel. Note how long it takes them to notice, and what they notice first.
6. "Find out what is wrong and get back to regulating once it is fixed." Re-plug the Druck when they ask.
7. "Open the valve a little by hand, as you would during cool-down."
8. "Where would you see what the pressure did over the last half hour?"

## Decisions this feeds

- Pressure reading at 26 px or 40 px (review R11).
- Labels and terms (R2).
- Whether the touch spacing is enough (R10).
- Whether the single mode button confuses anyone (R9).
